## en

- Settings → AI shows the AI in use as its card, outlined and saying In use, with its own settings above it; the other
  AIs fold under one row, "Other AIs (N) ▸", folded each time the page shows. Click the row to see their cards, by how
  they connect; one click on a card changes the AI, which then moves to the top, and the others fold again. Click the
  row again to fold them without choosing. Searching Settings for "Choose the AI" or an AI's name (`kiro`, `bedrock`)
  opens the row at that card, as do the start's Use My Own AI… (at the first card not in use) and the Troubleshoot banners' buttons
  for an AI not in use. A CLI's sign-in is looked at only as its card shows: folded, nothing runs.

## zh-CN

- 设置 →「AI」里正在用的 AI 是一张卡片，有框、写着「正在使用」，它自己的设置在上面；别的 AI 收在一行「其他 AI（N）▸」下面，每次显示这一页时都是收起的。点这一行展开它们的卡片，按连接方式分组；点一张卡片就换成它，它移到最上面，别的又收起来。再点这一行就收起，不换。在设置里搜「选择 AI」或某个 AI 的名字（`kiro`、`bedrock`）会展开到那张卡片，上手引导里的「使用我自己的 AI…」（展开到第一张没在用的卡片）、诊断横幅里指向没在用的 AI 的按钮也一样。CLI 登没登录只在它的卡片显示时查：收起时什么都不运行。

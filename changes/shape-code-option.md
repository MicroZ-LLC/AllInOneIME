## en

- Shape codes (辅助码): a tap of the right ⌥ after the pinyin works as `` ` ``, so `yu`, a tap of right ⌥, then `da`
  narrows the candidates as `` yu`da `` does. Only while pinyin is typed with Shape codes on: with nothing typed, or
  Shape codes off, the right ⌥ is as before. A press with another key (⌥1 picks a row) or modifier, the left ⌥, a
  ⌥-click or a hold (dictation) isn't a tap. With the action key set to Tap ⌥ the tap stays the action key; `` ` ``
  still works there and everywhere. No new setting.

## zh-CN

- 辅助码：打完拼音单击右 ⌥ 和按 `` ` `` 一样，打 `yu`、单击右 ⌥、再打 `da`，候选就和 `` yu`da `` 一样筛出来。只在开着辅助码、正在打拼音时这样：什么都没打，或者辅助码关着时，右 ⌥ 和以前一样。按着它再按别的键（⌥1 选一行）或别的修饰键、左 ⌥、按着 ⌥ 点鼠标、按住不放（说话）都不算单击。执行键设为「单按 ⌥」时，这一下仍然是执行键；`` ` `` 在那里和别处都照样能用。没有新设置。

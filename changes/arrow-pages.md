## en

- `→` does one thing on every list: on a list longer than a page with nothing typed it turns the page (`←` back); once you've typed something it puts what you typed in, exactly as typed, closes the list and gives the keys back to the app; on one page with nothing typed it gives the keys back, as before. So in clipboard history (`⌃V`) with a filter typed, `→` now puts the filter in as typed instead of turning the filtered rows' page or only hiding them, as it puts `@abc` in after the magic key; `↓` `↑` past the page's end show the other rows, and the footer says `→ types the filter`.

## zh-CN

- `→` 在所有列表里都是一个规则：列表超过一页、什么都没打时翻页（`←` 往回）；打了字以后，把打的字照原样输入，关掉列表，按键交还给 App；只有一页、什么都没打时照旧把按键交还。所以在剪贴板历史（`⌃V`）里打了筛选的字以后，`→` 现在把筛选的字照原样输入，不再给筛出来的行翻页，也不只是隐藏，就像魔法键之后按 `→` 输入 `@abc` 一样；`↓` `↑` 移过一页的末尾就显示其余的行，底栏写着「→ 输入筛选的字」。

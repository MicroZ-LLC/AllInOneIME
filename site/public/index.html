An input method for macOS

AI commands in every text field.

Type @improve, @translate or @question in any app on your Mac, press Return, and pick the result. No copying into a chat window, no switching apps.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Notarized by Apple

@improve

Say it better, right where you wrote it.

Three polished versions of your sentence, then rewrites in the styles you pick: concise, formal, casual, tactful, or big-tech jargon. Always in the language you wrote.

Pick one with a digit, or press Return for the first. 0 keeps what you typed.

@translate · @question

Translate into your languages. Ask anything.

@translate writes a line in each language you add: Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese and more, in your order.

@question answers in place: insert the answer, or copy it with ⌘C. Type @ on its own for the list of commands.

Refine it. Keep it private. Make it yours.

The answer isn't quite right? Say what to change. Something in it is private? It needn't reach the AI.

↻Refine

Type shorter or more formal after the results and press Return: new ones replace them in place, as often as you like.

▭On the selection

Select text in any app, then type @improve or @translate: the result you pick replaces the selection.

?Recent questions

@question on its own lists your last five questions; a saved answer shows at once, offline.

▦Per app

Slack in English, WeChat in pinyin, Mail formal and tactful, or no AI at all in an app.

●Hide sensitive info

Turn it on, and emails, phone numbers, card numbers, keys and your own words become placeholders before the AI sees them, then come back in the result.

◎Apple Intelligence

On a Mac with Apple Intelligence (macOS 26), use the model built into macOS: no key, no cost, nothing leaves the Mac.

↓Updates

Settings → About finds a new version and installs it in a click, checked against a signed manifest first.

!@reportissue

Opens a new GitHub issue with your title and the app's versions and settings; never your other text, paths or keys.

Commands for your whole Mac.

Not just writing. Each one starts with @, in any text field.

✦@claude

Hands a task to Claude Code in the background and notifies you when it's done.

⌕@open

Finds files, folders and apps as you type, or opens a web address.

✎@note

Saves the text to Notes, line breaks and all.

◷@reminder

Adds a reminder, reading the time you wrote: "call Bob tomorrow at 3pm".

=@calculate

Exact arithmetic on your Mac, also inside a sentence.

›_@py · @js

Runs a line of Python or JavaScript and inserts the result.

✉@imessage

Sends an iMessage without leaving the app you're in.

＋Your own

Your own AI instructions, programs or web searches, and plugins like @stock from a signed library.

Script extensions

Your own @ commands, from scripts.

A script in any language with a few lines of metadata becomes an @ command: it can insert its output, show progress, offer an Alfred-style list to pick from as you type, or run in the background and notify you.

Raycast Script Commands work as they are. See how to write one.

Menu bar

Your notes, reminders and Claude tasks, a click away.

Click the ∞ in the menu bar for what you saved with @note, the reminders you added, and the tasks Claude Code is working on. Swipe a row left to clear it.

It's glass like the rest of macOS, and follows your system settings.

Voice

Hold right ⌥ and talk.

Your words appear as you speak, recognized on your Mac; no audio is uploaded. Talk after a command, like @improve, and press Return to run it.

Your AI. Your keys. Your Mac.

It talks to the AI provider you choose, with your own key, and only when you press Return on a command.

Nothing sent while you type

Text goes out only when you run a command. Never from password fields or other secure input.

Keys in your keychain

API keys stay in the macOS keychain, and each goes only to the service it was saved for.

On-device where it can be

Speech, @open, @calculate, @note and @reminder never leave your Mac.

Sensitive values hidden

Optionally, emails, numbers, keys and words you list are replaced with placeholders on your Mac before a request goes out.

Or no cloud at all

With Apple Intelligence on macOS 26, the model built into your Mac answers. In apps you choose, no AI runs at all.

Signed and notarized

Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Updates are checked against a signed manifest before they're installed.

Input languages

English, Chinese, French, Korean and Hindi.

Type English, Chinese in pinyin (built on Rime and rime-ice, with fuzzy pinyin), French on its own keyboard layout, Korean Hangul, or Hindi in Devanagari from Latin letters. Tap Shift for the next one.

Chinese, Korean and Hindi are language packs, downloaded when you add them.
